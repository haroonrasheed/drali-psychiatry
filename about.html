About

Kishwar Ali, MD – Consultant Psychiatrist

Independent psychiatrist with 39 years of clinical experience providing respectful, confidential care for adults and families in Aurora, Illinois.

Biography

Dedicated to patient-centered psychiatric care.

Kishwar Ali, MD is an independent medical practitioner specializing in psychiatry. She provides careful evaluations, medication management, and ongoing follow-up with a focus on safety, trust, and collaboration.

Dr. Ali speaks English, Hindi, and Urdu, and emphasizes respectful communication, confidentiality, and evidence-based treatment tailored to each person.

Clinical experience

Clinical philosophy

Calm, ethical, and confidential care.
