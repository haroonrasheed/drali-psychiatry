About

Kishwar Ali, MD - Consultant Psychiatrist

Independent psychiatrist with 30 years of clinical experience providing respectful, confidential care for adults and families in Aurora, Illinois.

Biography

Dedicated to patient-centered psychiatric care.

Kishwar Ali, MD is an independent medical practitioner specializing in psychiatry. She provides careful evaluations, medication management, and ongoing follow-up with a focus on safety, trust, and collaboration.

Dr. Ali speaks English, Hindi, and Urdu, and emphasizes respectful communication, confidentiality, and evidence-based treatment tailored to each person.

Clinical experience

30 years providing psychiatric care for adults and families.

Education

Sindh Medical College, University of Karachi - 1986.

Residency

Loyola University Medical Center - 1998.

Practice

Independent psychiatrist (not employed by Endeavor Health Medical Group or Endeavor Health Hospitals).

Clinical philosophy

Calm, ethical, and confidential care.

Care plans are collaborative, evidence-based, and paced to your comfort. Visits end with clear next steps and how to reach the clinic between appointments.

Respect & privacy

Information is handled confidentially with a commitment to patient dignity.

In-person & telepsychiatry

Office visits in Aurora and secure video appointments when appropriate.

Clear communication

Plain-language explanations, medication safety checks, and coordinated follow-up.
