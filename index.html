NeuroStar TMS

What is NeuroStar TMS?

Medication may not work for everyone. NeuroStar TMS is a safe, non-invasive in-office treatment without the side effects commonly associated with antidepressants. Call our office today to learn more about this alternative treatment option for depression and OCD (Obsessive Compulsive Disorder)!

No Procedure

TMS therapy is a painless and non-surgical procedure. No anesthesia is required.

No Drug Side Effects

TMS Therapy is completely drug-free. After a session is over, you can confidently return to any regular daily activity.

Covered By Insurance

Most insurance companies will help cover the cost of your NeuroStar TMS therapy. Find out more by contacting us today.
