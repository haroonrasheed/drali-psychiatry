Conditions

Conditions treated

Reassuring, non-judgmental care for common mental health concerns. Assessments and treatment are tailored to each person with attention to safety and privacy.

Common concerns

Areas of focus

Schedule

Anxiety disorders

Persistent worry, panic, or tension affecting daily life.

Depression

Low mood, loss of interest, or changes in sleep and energy.

Bipolar disorder

Support for mood shifts with careful monitoring and follow-up.

OCD

Intrusive thoughts or repetitive behaviors that feel hard to control.

ADHD

Support for attention, focus, and executive function challenges across daily life.

PTSD & trauma-related stress

Distressing memories, hypervigilance, or burnout after difficult events.

Sleep disorders

Difficulty falling or staying asleep that affects focus and mood.

Stress-related conditions

Strain from work, family, or major life changes.

Life transitions

Support for adjustments impacting mental health and daily functioning.

Eating disorders

Thoughtful psychiatric support for eating-related concerns and recovery planning.
